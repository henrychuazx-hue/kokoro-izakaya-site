## Scope of work
- Target audience: **Primary 4** pupils (content maturity pegged to ages **11–12**).
- Language: **Mandarin** as used across Singapore MOE schools.
- **Voice-over: HUMAN child talent** (one child, male or female). **AI voice is prohibited.**
- **Duration: up to 3 min** — up to **2.5 min** content + **optional Q&A** segment (draft questions from WSHC; no interactivity needed).
- Style: **3D AI animation**, from the most suitable AI tools; **more sophisticated than the 2D sample** provided.
- Storyboard in **English** + accompanying **Chinese** script (client provides draft EN + CN scripts). Script edits must be approved by WSHC and keep meaning coherent.
- **Up to 3 review rounds** on the storyboard (before endorsement) **and up to 3** on the video.
- Contractor manages all **AI-tool copyright & licensing**; sources/clears any additional footage/images.
- **All IP (Foreground IP) vests in TAL.**
