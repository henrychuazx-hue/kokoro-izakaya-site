## Scene 1 — Beware of Blind Spots 【注意盲点】

**CN:** 一名女孩穿过停车场时，东西掉了，蹲下来捡。司机准备开出车位，两人都看不到彼此。幸好一名男孩（安全英雄）及时阻止了司机。事后，女孩意识到：在盲区里，不能假设别人看得到你。要先停下来，确认四周安全，才继续移动。旁白点出：不是每次都会有人在旁边帮你，**保护自己是自己的责任**。

**EN:** A child is cutting through a car park when they drop something and crouch to pick it up. A driver is about to pull out of a lot — neither can see the other. A passer-by (a "safety hero") spots the situation and stops the driver just in time. Afterwards the child takes it in. VO lands the message: you can't always count on someone being there. In blind spots — stop, check, then move. **Your safety is your own responsibility.**

> Note: the client's own EN says "a boy" while the CN says 女孩 (a girl). To confirm with WSHC.

## Scene 2 — Stop the Mobile, Safety First 【安全第一，过马路不使用手机】

**CN:** 男孩在斑马线前停下，左右查看车况。这时一名中学生走过来，边走边看手机，到了斑马线也没有停下。男孩叫住他，提醒他要先确认车辆停下才能过马路。突然，一辆摩托车从他身边疾驰而过，中学生这才惊吓抬起头。男孩补充：过马路一直看手机是非常危险的事情——你根本不知道周围发生了什么事。

**EN:** The boy stops at the zebra crossing and checks both ways. A secondary-school student walks past, eyes on his phone, and steps onto the crossing without stopping. The boy calls out and tells him to wait until the cars have stopped. Suddenly a motorcyclist dashes past and the teen looks up, shocked. The boy adds: being on your phone doesn't just put you at risk — it means you have no idea what's happening around you at all.

## Scene 3 — LOOK. THINK. DO 【一看、二想、三行动】

**CN:** 男孩走到红绿灯处，人行灯还剩 3 秒。对面有人看到倒数，选择冲过马路。一辆正在转弯的车紧急刹车。镜头聚焦司机——先是震惊，然后是愤怒，最后是后怕，双手还在颤抖。再切回那个冲过马路的人——他站在路边，胸口起伏，腿有点软，回头看了一眼那辆车，表情凝重。

**EN:** The boy reaches a signalised crossing. The pedestrian light shows 3 seconds. Across the road, someone sees the countdown and runs for it. A turning car brakes hard. The camera holds on the driver — shock, then anger, then a wave of relief, hands still trembling. Cut to the person who ran: on the kerb, breathing hard, legs unsteady, glancing back with a pale, shaken expression.

## Ending — Care for One Another 【互相关心】

**CN:** 除了遵守规则，观察情况和环境以做出最安全的行动也非常重要。一看、二想、三行动——保护自己与照顾他人。如果发生事故，在必要时**见义勇为**，拨打 **995** 启动**民防部队**拯救生命。

**EN:** Beyond following the rules, observing the situation and environment to take the safest action matters. **Look. Think. Do** — protect yourself and take care of others. If an accident happens, be courageous when needed and call **995** to activate the **SCDF** to save lives.
