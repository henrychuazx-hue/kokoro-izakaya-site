## Confirmed cast (locked in Step 1)
- **Boy** — the hero / endangered child (Scene 1: crouches to pick something up in the car park). Matches the WSHC EN script ("a boy"; note CN says 女孩 — confirm with WSHC). **Expression sheet** created for consistency.
- **Uncle** — the adult passer-by / safety hero who stops the reversing driver in time (the film's first 见义勇为). **Expression sheet** created.
- **Zebra Mascot** — the guardian. Anthropomorphic zebra; the freeze-and-reveal device; wink-from-a-distance close.
- **Van** — key prop; dedicated **van prop sheet** (used a lot in the blind-spot scene).

## Demo production pipeline (as documented)
1. **Step 1 — Lock character sheets** (Boy, Uncle, Zebra Mascot) + expression sheets + van prop sheet.
2. **Step 2 — Core environment plates**, fixed up in **Photoshop** to serve as anchors (makes corrections cheaper/consistent):
   - Exterior: **Side view · Street level · Overhead**
   - In-vehicle: **Dashboard view · Cargo view**
3. **Step 3 — Prompt sequences** using key elements + scene anchors; **mix and match multiple takes** for the best result. (Demo built as Sequences 1–3, ~3–5 shots each.)
4. **Step 3b — Post:** sequences edited, stitched and colour-corrected; **audio kept on separate tracks** for further tweaking and voice-over.

## Completed sample scene (the .mov in this repo)
- Scene 1 "Beware of Blind Spots" (注意盲点) — **~29 seconds, 1280×720 (720p)**, 8.6 MB.
- File: `../02-source-materials/sample-video/Sample_Scene_Blind_Spots.mov`.
- ⚠️ For final delivery the brief needs high-res for large projector screens — **target ≥1080p (up to 4K)**; the 720p demo is a proof-of-style, not the delivery spec.

## Style
- **Painterly 3D**, warm painted finish, more sophisticated than a flat 2D cartoon; bright and child-appealing with weight at the safety beats (red reversing lights, frozen dust, gold freeze shimmer).
