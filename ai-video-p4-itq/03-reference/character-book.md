## Cast to design (from the proposal & WSHC script)
1. **Hero — the child** (Primary-4-aged, ~9–11). Relatable, everyday; the one who *acts* and saves themselves. Must be **recognisably consistent across every shot** (continuity is the whole point).
2. **Zebra Guardian — 安全守护者.** Anthropomorphic zebra; calm, warm, a little wondrous. A nod to the **zebra crossing** and "look both ways." He **reveals**, never rescues. Signature beats: appears at the freeze; **wink-from-a-distance** to close. Designed as a **recurring series mascot** for future safety topics.
3. **Supporting:** peer "safety hero"; phone-distracted secondary student; drivers; **motorcyclist (摩托车骑士)**; vehicles (reversing van / turning car); car-park, zebra-crossing and signalised-junction environments.

## Style
- **Painterly 3D**, warm painted finish, closer to feature animation than flat TV cartoon; bright and child-appealing but with real weight at the safety beats (red reversing lights, frozen dust, gold freeze shimmer).
- **More sophisticated than the 2D sample** referenced in the ITQ.

## For the character sheet (deliverable)
- Hero + guardian: front/side/expression turnarounds, colour call-outs, scale/height reference, wardrobe & palette locked (Cast & Asset Bible, methodology §8 step 2).
