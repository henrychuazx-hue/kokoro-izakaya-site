# Creative Review — ITQ-FY26-WS-008 "Guardian Crossing" Proposal

*Produced by the creative-director review agent against `01-proposal/ITQ_Proposal_v1.md` + the WSHC script. Findings integrated into the current proposal; this file is the QA record.*

**Verdicts:**
- **Guardian device + fidelity:** threading all three scenes is the right call; "freeze → reveal the unseen" is a strong retention device. But the original framing "the child saves themselves / 保护自己是自己的责任" **contradicted Scene 1** (a peer hero does the saving) and flattened the script's **dual** message (protect yourself **and** 照顾他人/见义勇为). *(Fixed in §1, §5.)*
- **Age arc:** intent right (real stakes, no trauma) but age was misstated "nine-to-eleven" vs the brief's 11–12. *(Fixed throughout.)*
- **~100-word write-up:** length fine, but over-indexed Scene 1 + the freeze and dropped the 见义勇为/995 ending. *(Replaced in §3.)*

## Fixes applied
**P1 — fidelity/logic**
1. "Child saves themselves" contradiction → re-centered on 一看、二想、三行动; carries both 保护自己 **and** 见义勇为. *(§1, §5)*
2. Scene 2 dropped the hero's active warning → restored ("calls out to warn the teen to wait until cars stop"). *(§5.1)*
3. Sentence pattern 「一个人…，另一个人…」 was listed but never placed → anchored in Scene 1 VO. *(§5.2)*
4. Vocabulary inconsistency + ending crammed with ambulance/injury words → added a **complete vocab-coverage map** (§5.2); moved 包扎伤口/记者采访/获得表扬 to a textbook-tag/Q&A beat, not an injury visual.
5. ~100-word write-up rewritten to name all three scenes, two distinct highlights, and the dual lesson. *(§3)*

**P2 — consistency / age**
6. Age unified to 11–12. *(§4, §5)*
7. §5/§6 rewritten: three escalating vignettes; the "three highlights" reframed as three **techniques** recurring across scenes.
8. Timing budget added for three freezes (one ~12–15s hero freeze, shorter ~5–8s reveals). *(§6)*
9. "No trauma" backed explicitly: no collision/injury on screen; Scene 3 resolves on relief. Guardian rendered cinematic/understated for maturity. *(§5)*
10. Scene 1 rescue tagged as the film's first 见义勇为 so the ending pays off as a callback. *(§5.1)*

**P3 — polish / over-claim**
11. Orphaned "fourteen shots" number removed. *(§7)*
12. Bottleneck thesis (was repeated 3×) stated once. *(§7)*
13. "Back-catalogue of cartoons" softened; mascot reframed as **reusable** for future WSHC/MOE topics (IP vests in TAL), i.e. value for money not a franchise grab. *(§1, §5)*
14. Scene 1 casting discrepancy (CN 女孩 vs EN "a boy") → note added that gender is confirmed with WSHC at storyboard round 1 and kept continuous with the sample. *(§5)*
