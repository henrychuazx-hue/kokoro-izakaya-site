The part a file scan can't see

Clean files. The risk is what they read.

1  Reads a stranger's words
Pulls captions and transcripts from 8 to 12 other creators' channels through Sandcastles. Anyone can write an instruction into a caption.
2  Sees your private stuff
Gmail, Drive and Calendar connected to the same Claude account.
3  Can send things out
Tool calls, web requests, email drafts.
All three in one chat is where prompt injection gets dangerous. Switch the other connectors off.
