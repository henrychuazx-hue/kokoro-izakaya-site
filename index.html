FAQ

  

  

    

      Does it really work on a modern flat-screen TV?
      
Yes — that's the whole point. Classic light guns needed CRT timing tricks; RetroTrigger uses four clip-on IR beacons and triangulates the muzzle position instead. LED, OLED, QLED and projectors up to 100" all work. Calibration takes about 30 seconds, once.

    

    

      Do I need a console or PC?
      
You need a host — and you probably already own one: any Windows PC, a Batocera or Raspberry Pi build, or an Android box/TV. Plug the USB receiver into the host, clip the IR positioning light to your screen, calibrate once, shoot. The pistols speak standard USB light-gun protocols the emulator and PC gaming community supports. No games are included with the pistols.

    

    

      Which grip fit should I choose?
      
Standard is the full frame — if you're comfortable with a full-size pistol grip or a large gaming mouse, take it. Compact shortens the trigger reach and slims the grip by roughly 8–10%; it suits smaller hands, and plenty of people simply prefer the tighter hold for long sessions. It's a sizing choice, not a gendered one, and it costs the same. Not sure? Measure from the base of your palm to the tip of your middle finger: under ~18cm, start with Compact.

    

    

      What's the difference between the loadouts?
      
DEADEYE is the standard pistol with 12V solenoid recoil — the kick you remember from the cabinet. DEADEYE ELITE is the same pistol with the hard case, IR bar stand, a spare pair of cells and a numbered calibration card. GODMODE SET is two recoil pistols on one receiver for couch co-op, and you can mix editions and fits across the pair. Every loadout has identical tracking hardware — you're choosing recoil, extras and player count, not accuracy.

    

    

      How does the $49 deposit work?
      
Your deposit reserves a unit in the batch and locks your price. We invoice the balance by email before the batch ships — pay it then, or cancel any time before dispatch and the deposit is refunded in full, instantly, no questions.

    

    

      When will my pre-order ship?
      
Units ship in numbered batches. When your batch closes, production is locked, every pistol is QC test-fired, and tracking numbers go out within 5 days of batch close. Typical door-to-door is 2–4 weeks after that — the current window is shown above the pre-order button.

    

    

      Where do you ship, and what does it cost?
      
Shipping regions and any shipping cost are shown at the Stripe checkout before you pay — no surprises after the fact. Every crate ships insured with door-to-door tracking.

    

    

      What's the recoil actually like?
      
A solenoid kick plus rumble, synced per shot — closer to an arcade cabinet gun than a rumbling gamepad. Strong enough to be the reason the neighbors knock.

    

    

      What if it arrives damaged or I change my mind?
      
30-day no-drama returns and a 12-month warranty on every unit. Shipping is insured and tracked; if a courier folds your crate in half, we replace it — you never fight the courier, we do.

    

    

      Is my payment safe?
      
Checkout runs entirely on Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processor trusted by millions of businesses. We never see or store your card details. You're charged once at order, and every order gets an instant email receipt.
